Who I Am

I’ve always been drawn to design, technology, and the way the internet connects people and businesses.

For a long time, I never understood why so many good businesses struggled or disappeared while the internet gave access to billions of people around the world.

Eventually, I realized the problem wasn’t always the business, many simply weren’t visible online, and those that were often felt outdated, confusing, or difficult to reach.

That’s what led me into web design. I fell in love with helping businesses build an online presence that people can actually find, trust, and connect with.
